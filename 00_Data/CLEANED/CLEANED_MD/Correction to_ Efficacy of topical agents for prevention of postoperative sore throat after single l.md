In the article entitled: "Efficacy of topical agents for prevention of postoperative sore throat after single lumen tracheal intubation: a Bayesian network meta-analysis"
published online and in the November print issue of the Journal, Can J Anesth 2020; 67: 1624-42, "DIC" should be defined as "deviance information criterion" (instead of "disseminated intravascular coagulation").
